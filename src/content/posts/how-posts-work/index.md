Each post is a patch note. Say what changed, what broke, and what we think about it. No SEO titles. No "we're thrilled to announce."

```notes
+ added: what shipped
- removed: what we killed
~ changed: what we're unsure about
! broke: what broke, and whose fault (ours)
```

Projects that die get a post too, tagged `dead`, with a cause of death. Half the value of a devlog is seeing what didn't make it.
